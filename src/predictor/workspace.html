    A WORKFLOW FOR A MARKETER

    From an observation to a decision.

    01 — 03
    01

    Bring real activity.

    Use one row per browsing session from your analytics export. Enter the values manually or put them in the CSV template. This site does not automatically connect to your store.

    02

    Read the prediction.

    Get Purchase predicted or No purchase predicted, a score, and the inputs that influenced it. Your own data goes through a real trained model; no fixed customer list is being replayed.

    03

    Review, then act.

    Use the ranked list to prioritise campaign or outreach review. Confirm with your store’s own outcomes before automating spend. A high score does not confirm a sale.
